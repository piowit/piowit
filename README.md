## About Me 📌

Hi there! Before dedicating myself fully to software engineering, I spent 9 incredible years as a motion graphics designer, where I honed my leadership skills as a team lead for three of those years. In 2022, I made a full commercial transition to the tech world, closing the motion graphics chapter. However, my love for programming dates back to when I was just 13 years old. From building webpages, CMS, and forums, to modifying games, my curiosity in the tech realm has always been relentless.

Now, I'm deeply immersed in full-stack and backend development. My core expertise lies in Ruby, Ruby on Rails, Hotwire, and JavaScript, but my daily toolkit has recently expanded to include **Astro and Strapi**, alongside Next.js, WebSockets, Docker workflows, and AI-assisted development tools (like Claude Code & MCP servers). I'm passionate about the benefits of TDD, CI/CD, and smooth deployments, always eager to learn more each day.

Currently, I work as an independent software consultant collaborating with multiple companies. I am an expert in **system integrations and building robust integration frameworks**, helping businesses seamlessly connect complex architectures and streamline their operations.

A few fun facts about me:

- 🌍 I have an insatiable wanderlust. Whether it's a camping road trip across the Balkans in a VW T4 or flying to Japan, I love exploring the world in unconventional ways.
- 🐈 Passionate about all creatures great and small, I share my home with a black cat.
- 🎵 Music is my outlet! In my free time, you might find me strumming my guitar (acoustic or electric), playing the piano, or taking singing lessons.
- 🛠️ I'm a tinkerer at heart. Offline, I enjoy tweaking cars and fixing motorcycles (like my BMW R1100GS). Online, I'm obsessed with my homelab, running Home Assistant and self-hosting services.
- 🌱 Gardening brings me peace, and I love taking care of my indoor palms.
- 👯 I’m open to collaborating on anything intriguing!
- 📚 Teaching is another passion of mine. I've given multiple workshops about Hotwire at Ruby Community Conferences.
- ❤️ I'm also developing small applications with my wife, [Kaja](https://github.com/kajawitek), who is a solid mid-level software developer!
